# Photo grid prompts

The newspaper and notice photos were generated with an image AI (ChatGPT), nine at a time, as a single 3×3 grid that is then cut apart (see `tools/`). The original grids and the cut photos are in [`../photos/`](../photos/).

To make a new grid, paste the common part below, followed by a list of nine photos.

## Common part

```
Please generate ONE single image that contains a grid of 9 separate
newspaper press photographs, arranged in 3 rows and 3 columns.

LAYOUT (very important):
- The whole image is landscape, 3:2 aspect ratio (for example 1536x1024).
- 3 rows x 3 columns, all 9 cells exactly the same size.
- Each cell is its own separate photo, also 3:2 landscape.
- Thin plain white gaps between the cells and a thin white border around
  the whole image, so the photos can be cut apart cleanly.
- Order: left to right, top to bottom, photo 1 is top left, photo 9 is
  bottom right.
- No numbers, captions or titles in the cells or in the gaps.

STYLE FOR EVERY PHOTO:
- A realistic documentary press photo, as if taken by a newspaper
  photographer, not an illustration or a drawing.
- Black and white, slightly grainy film, natural light, some contrast,
  like a photo printed on the front page of a newspaper.
- No text, letters, numbers, logos, symbols, charts, brand names or
  watermarks anywhere. All screens, labels, signs and packaging must be
  blank or show only plain light.
- No recognizable real people, celebrities or real companies.
- Every scene is shot completely seriously and looks newsworthy, even
  when the subject is absurd. The humour comes from the seriousness.

THE 9 PHOTOS:
```

Labels such as `KD-PX` on crates are added afterwards; image AIs tend to misspell text.
